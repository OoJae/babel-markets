Manoma a jihar Kano sun ce farashin rogo zai iya tashi a wannan shekara saboda yanayin damina mara kyau. Wani manomi a kasuwar Dawanau ya ce farashin tan daya na rogo ya tashi daga Naira 180,000 zuwa Naira 230,000 a watanni shida da suka wuce. Babu cikakken bayani game da yawan ruwan sama da aka samu a wannan kakar. Masu fataucin kayan abinci na ganin cewa farashin zai ci gaba da tashi amma ba a tabbatar da hakan ba.
